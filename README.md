# Influencer-Marketing
The influence of social media influencer on the customers
--- 
# Research Hypotheses

H1. The Perfection Paradox
Influencer attractiveness has an inverted U-shaped relationship with consumer purchase intention, suggesting that excessively high attractiveness may reduce purchase intention.

H2. The Expertise Paradox
Perceived similarity moderates the relationship between influencer expertise and consumer purchase intention. Specifically, the positive effect of expertise on purchase intention weakens when perceived similarity is high.

H3. The Authenticity Paradox
The negative effect of perceived commercialism on consumer purchase intention is stronger for influencers with higher levels of prior perceived authenticity.

H4. The Trust–Purchase Gap
The positive relationship between influencer trustworthiness and consumer purchase intention weakens as perceived product risk increases.

H5. The AI Influencer Paradox
The effect of influencer type (human vs. AI-generated influencer) on consumer purchase intention is moderated by product category.

### H6. LLM vs. Human Consumer Responses
LLM-generated synthetic consumer responses systematically overestimate the positive effect of influencer credibility on purchase intention compared with real human survey data.
